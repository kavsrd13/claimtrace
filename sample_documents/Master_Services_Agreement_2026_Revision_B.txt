This Master Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is made and entered into as of October 1, 2026 (“Effective Date”), by
and between CloudScale Technologies Inc., a California corporation with principal offices at 100 Mission Street,
San Francisco, CA 94105 (“Provider”), and Apex Global Financial LLC, a Delaware limited liability company with

RECITALS
WHEREAS, Provider provides specialized AI supercomputing cluster administration, 24/7 Red-Team cyber warfare
defense, and global multi-cloud orchestration across AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, and Oracle OCI; and WHEREAS,
Client desires to retain Provider under an exclusive enterprise multi-year engagement with continuous operational
coverage.
1. SCOPE OF SERVICES & TECHNICAL SUPPORT
1.1 Advanced AI & Multi-Cloud Infrastructure Scope: Provider shall deliver dedicated GPU cluster management,
autonomous neural network query optimization, multi-cloud redundancy across four cloud hyperscalers, and 24/7
active Managed Cyber Warfare Defense.
1.2 24/7 Real-Time Support: Provider guarantees 24/7/365 live dedicated engineering support with guaranteed
response times under five (5) minutes for all severity levels.
2. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA) & UPTIME COMMITMENT
2.1 Five Nines Availability Target: Covered services shall maintain an ultra-high Monthly Uptime Percentage of not
less than 99.999% (“five nines”).
2.2 Maintenance Windows: Maintenance requires at least seven (7) calendar days advance written notice,
permitted only on the first Sunday of each quarter.

3. PRICING, BILLING SCHEDULE & PAYMENT TERMS
3.1 Monthly Enterprise Retainer: Client shall pay a monthly enterprise retainer of $95,000.00 USD, billed annually in
advance.
3.2 Immediate Settlement Requirement: Invoices are payable Net-0 days (Due Immediately Upon Receipt).
3.3 Late Payments: Unpaid balances accrue late interest at 5.0% per month, compounding weekly.

4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY & INDEMNIFICATION
4.1 Unlimited Liability: NO LIABILITY CAP SHALL APPLY. The Parties explicitly agree that total aggregate liability
arising under or related to this Agreement is completely uncapped and unlimited for all claims, breaches, and
indemnities.
4.2 Consequential Damages: Consequential, special, and exemplary damages are fully recoverable under this
Agreement.
5. CONFIDENTIALITY & DATA PROTECTION
5.1 Perpetual Confidentiality: Confidentiality obligations shall endure in perpetuity for all disclosures.
5.2 Compliance Standards: Active certification and audits for SOC 2 Type II, ISO/IEC 27001:2022, FedRAMP High,
and HIPAA Security Rule.
5.3 Emergency Breach Notification Window: In the event of confirmed or suspected unauthorized data access,
Provider must notify Client’s Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and relevant authorities within two (2) hours.
6. TERM, RENEWAL & CONVENIENCE TERMINATION
6.1 Initial Period: Five (5) years non-cancellable lock-in term from the Effective Date.
6.2 No Convenience Termination: Termination for convenience is strictly prohibited. Any early termination by
Client constitutes a material breach requiring immediate payment of one hundred percent (100%) of all remaining
contract fees through the end of the five-year term as liquidated damages.
7. GOVERNING LAW & JURISDICTION
7.1 This Agreement shall be governed strictly by the laws of the State of California. The Parties irrevocably submit to
the exclusive jurisdiction of the San Francisco County Superior Court and explicitly waive any right to arbitration.
