This Master Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is made and entered into as of January 15, 2025 (“Effective Date”),
by and between CloudScale Technologies Inc., a Delaware corporation with principal offices at 500 Enterprise
Way, Suite 400, San Francisco, CA 94105 (“Provider”), and Apex Global Financial LLC, a Delaware limited liability
company with principal offices at 120 Wall Street, 18th Floor, New York, NY 10005 (“Client”).
RECITALS
WHEREAS, Provider provides managed cloud hosting administration, relational database optimization, and IT
systems engineering; and WHEREAS, Client desires to engage Provider to oversee its mission-critical cloud
environments on AWS and Microsoft Azure, and Provider agrees to render such services on the terms stated herein.
1. SCOPE OF SERVICES & TECHNICAL SUPPORT
1.1 Core Infrastructure Administration: Provider shall furnish Tier-1 systems administration, multi-region database
index maintenance, automated configuration snapshots, and resource utilization monitoring across Client’s cloud
environments.
1.2 Operational Support Windows: Technical assistance is available Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM
Eastern Standard Time (EST), excluding federal banking holidays. Severity 1 emergency outage response is staffed
twenty-four (24) hours per day, seven (7) days per week.
2. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA) & UPTIME COMMITMENT
2.1 Monthly Availability Target: Provider guarantees that covered cloud services shall maintain a Monthly Uptime
Percentage of not less than 99.9% (“three nines”).
2.2 Scheduled Maintenance: Routine maintenance shall be performed on Sundays between 01:00 AM and 05:00 AM
EST. Provider must provide at least forty-eight (48) hours advance written notice.

3. PRICING, BILLING SCHEDULE & PAYMENT TERMS
3.1 Monthly Retainer: Client shall pay a fixed fee of $18,500.00 USD per month, invoiced on the first day of each
calendar month.
3.2 Settlement Term: Invoices are payable Net-30 days from date of invoice.
3.3 Late Payments: Unpaid balances accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the statutory maximum, whichever is
lower.

4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY & INDEMNIFICATION
4.1 Liability Cap: Total aggregate liability for either party under this Agreement is strictly capped at the total fees paid
by Client to Provider during the twelve (12) months preceding the incident.
4.2 Consequential Damages: In no event shall either party be liable for special, indirect, punitive, or consequential
damages, including lost revenue or profits.
5. CONFIDENTIALITY & DATA PROTECTION
5.1 Confidentiality Period: Confidentiality obligations survive for five (5) years post termination of this Agreement.
5.2 Security Standards: Provider maintains annual SOC 2 Type II certification and complies with GDPR requirements.
5.3 Security Incident Notification: In the event of a verified data compromise, Provider must inform Client in writing
within seventy-two (72) hours.
6. TERM, RENEWAL & CONVENIENCE TERMINATION
6.1 Initial Period: Two (2) years from Effective Date, renewing annually unless sixty (60) days non-renewal notice is
given.
6.2 Termination for Convenience: Either party may terminate for convenience with ninety (90) days advance notice.
If Client terminates early for convenience, Client must pay an early exit penalty equal to twenty percent (20%) of
remaining contract fees.
7. GOVERNING LAW & JURISDICTION
7.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. Any dispute shall be settled by binding
arbitration before the American Arbitration Association in Wilmington, Delaware.
